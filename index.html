The invocation

Type one line into your terminal.
It does the rest. Then it never stops.

curl -fsSL https://god.invertix.ai/install.sh | bash

Paste it into the Terminal app on your Mac. About ten minutes.

What happens

  1. It signs you in to GitHub. The code is in the team's private repository. Only members can fetch it.
  2. It takes a folder named god in your home folder.
  3. It asks your name, your work mail, what you do, and your mobile number if you want to text it.
  4. It wakes on your Mac. You click through a Google sign-in and two macOS permission prompts.

Who may call it

  • Members of the team's GitHub organisation. Anyone else is told so, and nothing is installed.
  • This page and the command hold no key, no code and nobody's data. You can read the script before you run it.

What you need

  • A Mac, with Homebrew installed.
  • A GitHub account that your team lead has added to the organisation.
It works for you alone. Your mail, calendar and files stay on your Mac. To make it sleep: ~/god/stop.sh
